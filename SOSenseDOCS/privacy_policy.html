SOSense Official Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy & Data Safety

Effective Date: October 6, 2026 | Application Version: 1.0.3

🛡️ 1. Introduction & Overview

SOSense ("we", "our", or "the App"), developed under package ID com.sosence.app, is a personal emergency and safety response application. We hold user privacy and data sovereignty as fundamental priorities.

Our core operational principle is privacy-first, on-device local execution. SOSense does not sell, lease, or monetize your personal information or real-time location data.

📍 2. Information We Collect and How We Use It

SOSense gathers only the minimal required information necessary to execute emergency life-saving services:

Data Category Purpose Storage & Transfer
Precise GPS Location To attach your live Google Maps coordinates to emergency SOS alerts sent to your trusted contacts and display nearby safe zones (hospitals, police stations). Captured on-demand. Never uploaded to proprietary cloud servers. Dispatched only via user SMS.
Trusted Contacts List To store names and phone numbers of guardians and emergency responders who will receive your SOS messages. Encrypted and stored locally in on-device SQLite database.
Motion & Sensor Data Accelerometer and sensor signals used by the Fall Detection engine to recognize sudden falls or high-velocity impacts. Processed locally in real-time in memory. Discarded immediately after analysis.
Battery Status Monitors power level to trigger the 5% Critical Battery SOS alert so your family knows your phone is powering off. Read via standard Android battery broadcast receiver.
Emergency Medical Profile Blood type, emergency medical notes, and primary contact details for display on your local Emergency Card. Stored 100% locally on your smartphone.

🔑 3. Android Permissions & Justifications

SOSense requests standard runtime Android permissions strictly to fulfill core safety functions:

  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION & ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION: Calculates GPS coordinates during SOS events and renders local maps.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION: Ensures continuous location updates when an active emergency tracking session is running.
  • SEND_SMS: Transmits emergency alert text messages directly through your cellular carrier to designated contacts.
  • READ_CONTACTS: Allows you to pick contacts directly from your address book without manual typing.
  • BODY_SENSORS & ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION: Enables background fall detection and emergency response triggering.
  • VIBRATE & POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Delivers critical countdown alerts, haptic feedback, and active notification drawer quick actions.

🤖 4. Third-Party Services & APIs

To provide top-tier functionality, SOSense interfaces with trusted developer services:

  • Google Play Services Location & Maps: Provides location resolution and map rendering. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Gemini AI API: Powers the AI Safety Assistant. When you submit a safety query, your text prompt is transmitted via encrypted HTTPS to Google Gemini services to generate safety advice. No personal identity is linked to AI queries.
  • OpenStreetMap (osmdroid): Provides open-source map tile caching for offline map navigation.

🗑️ 5. Data Retention & User Data Deletion

Because all primary data is saved locally on your device:

  • You have complete control to view, modify, or delete trusted contacts and incident logs at any time via the Data Management or Settings screen in the app.
  • Tapping "Clear All Data" permanently deletes all stored SOS history, contacts, and configuration.
  • Uninstalling the SOSense application completely purges all application databases and preferences from your device.

👶 6. Children's Privacy

SOSense is designed for general audiences, families, teenagers, and seniors. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal identifiable information from children under 13 without parental consent.

8. Full Description
OneTapSOS is an advanced personal safety and emergency response application designed to protect you, your family, and loved ones in critical situations. Whether you are commuting late at night, traveling alone, facing an unexpected medical crisis, or encountering an unsafe situation, OneTapSOS gives you immediate access to life-saving tools right from your device.

KEY CAPABILITIES AND FEATURES:

1. Instant Emergency SOS Dispatch
Trigger an immediate SOS alert with a single tap, a 3-second hold, holding physical hardware volume keys, or activating the Quick Settings tile from your notification panel. OneTapSOS automatically captures your exact GPS location and broadcasts custom emergency SMS messages to all your pre-configured trusted contacts.

2. Real-Time Location Sharing & Live Tracking
Send an interactive Google Maps link with live coordinates and timestamps directly to your family and emergency contacts. Continuous foreground tracking keeps your safety circle updated with your movement during an active crisis.

3. Automated Fall Detection Engine
Using continuous accelerometer and motion sensor monitoring, OneTapSOS detects sudden high-impact falls and collisions. If a fall occurs and you are unresponsive, the system automatically initiates an emergency countdown and notifies your emergency circle.

4. Safe Map & Nearby Emergency Safe Zones
Discover nearby emergency infrastructure including hospitals, police stations, fire stations, and 24/7 pharmacies on an interactive map powered by OpenStreetMap and Google Maps API.

5. Critical Battery Warning
When your device battery level drops to 5 percent, OneTapSOS automatically alerts your chosen contacts with your last known location, ensuring your family is aware before your phone powers off.

6. OneTapSOS AI Safety Advisor
Powered by Google Gemini AI, get instant, practical guidance on risk assessment, emergency first-aid protocols, and crisis management steps during unpredictable scenarios.

7. Discrete Fake Call Simulation
Simulate realistic incoming phone calls with custom caller profiles and timers to discreetly exit uncomfortable or dangerous situations without escalating tension.

8. Safety Timer & Periodic Check-in
Set customizable safety countdown timers for late-night journeys or high-risk activities. If you fail to check in before the timer expires, an automated SOS alert is dispatched to your guardians.

9. Offline First Aid & Disaster Survival Guides
Access comprehensive, offline-ready instructions for CPR, bleeding control, burns, earthquakes, floods, and natural disaster preparedness anytime, anywhere.

10. Privacy-First On-Device Architecture
All contact lists, location logs, emergency history, and medical profile details are stored locally on your device in an encrypted SQLite database. No personal tracking data is uploaded to external servers without your explicit trigger.

11. Multi-Language Support
Full native interface localization for English, Hindi, and Tamil to ensure accessibility across diverse linguistic communities.

- Notifications & System (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, VIBRATE, BIND_QUICK_SETTINGS_TILE, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, WAKE_LOCK, REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS)

29. Explain why these permissions are required
- ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION: Required to attach your precise geographical coordinates and Google Maps link to outgoing emergency SOS alerts and to display nearby emergency facilities on the Safe Map.
- FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION: Required to maintain uninterrupted GPS tracking and location broadcast during an active emergency SOS session.
- SEND_SMS: Required to dispatch instant emergency alert text messages directly to your designated trusted contacts when SOS is triggered or low battery alert fires.
- READ_CONTACTS: Required to let users easily select and import trusted family members and guardians into their emergency contact list.
- BODY_SENSORS and ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION: Required for the accelerometer-based Fall Detection service to recognize sudden high-impact accidents and start emergency countdowns.
- CAMERA and RECORD_AUDIO: Required for emergency video/audio evidence logging and safety flashlight utility during crises.
- POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required to display ongoing safety status, active SOS notifications, safety timers, and low battery alerts.
- VIBRATE: Required to provide immediate haptic feedback confirming SOS activation, button holds, and danger countdown ticks.
- BIND_QUICK_SETTINGS_TILE: Required to allow users to trigger an emergency SOS directly from their Android notification drawer without unlocking the full app.
- RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Required to restart background safety monitoring, fall detection, and battery monitoring when the device reboots.
- SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM & WAKE_LOCK: Required to ensure safety check-in countdown timers fire accurately even when the device is sleeping.
- REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS: Required to prevent Android Doze mode from killing critical background emergency detection services.

38. Reviewer Instructions
Step-by-step test procedure for Google Play Reviewers:
1. Launch the application. Swipe through the Onboarding screens and tap "Get Started" to reach the Main Dashboard.
2. Add a Trusted Contact: Tap "Trusted Contacts" or "Vault", tap "Add Contact", and enter a test contact name and number.
3. Test SOS Trigger: Press and hold the central red "SOS" button for 3 seconds or tap the SOS Activation button. The 5-second cancel countdown will appear.
4. Verify Countdown & Resolution: Let the countdown finish (or tap Send Now). The SOS Active screen will display alert details. Tap "I'm Safe" to resolve the alert.
5. Test Safe Map: Tap "Safe Map" on the dashboard to view nearby hospitals and police stations on the interactive map.
6. Test AI Safety Assistant: Tap "AI Assistant" on the dashboard, type any safety question (e.g. "What should I do during an earthquake?"), and receive a real-time safety response from Google Gemini AI.
7. Test Settings & Diagnostics: Tap the Settings gear icon in the top header and select "SOS Diagnostics" to run a system self-check verifying SMS, Location, and DB integrity.
